One budget year did most of the building

2019 funded 713 of the 1,032 projects and 76% of the 56,639 units. Output fell to 112 projects in 2020 and just 12 in 2021, then recovered to 195 in 2022, when the median unit cost was also higher.

21%of units · 2% of projects

Jakarta builds tall; everyone else builds small

DKI Jakarta has only 24 projects but 12,134 units. A single 2019 multi-year contract (MYC) for low-income households accounts for 7,426 units, or 13% of the national total. Nationally the typical project is small, with a median of 42 units, and 81% of projects have 50 units or fewer.

Building in the east costs more

Across Papua, Papua Barat, Maluku, Maluku Utara and NTT, the median project cost Rp 393 juta per unit, against Rp 251 juta on Java. Maluku (Rp 478 juta) and Papua (Rp 447 juta) have the highest provincial medians. Riau (Rp 208 juta) has the lowest.

Who it's for shapes what it costs

The median santri project costs Rp 211 juta per unit, students Rp 252 juta and low-income households Rp 298 juta. Housing for the military and police (TNI/POLRI, Rp 358 juta) and civil servants (ASN, Rp 378 juta) costs the most. That fits larger family-sized units, but the dataset doesn't record floor area, so it can't confirm this.

Dataset

There are 1,032 project records, each with a location, a beneficiary label, a province, a budget year, the number of units and the total cost. In total they cover 56,639 units and Rp 15.85 triliun across 34 provinces.

Cost per unit

  • Project cost per unit = total cost ÷ units, in Rp juta (millions) in nominal terms, not adjusted for inflation.
  • Benchmarks. Each project is compared with the national median (Rp juta) and with the median of its own beneficiary group. Medians are used because a few very large or very cheap records pull the mean upward. The mean is Rp 293 juta, and the unit-weighted average is Rp 280 juta.
  • Map colour classes are ratios to the national median: under 0.75×, 0.75–0.9×, 0.9–1.1×, 1.1–1.33×, and 1.33× or more.

Caveats

  • Multi-year contracts. The Jakarta MYC records (2019–2021) may report only one year's allocation. The 2021 MYC-ASN record works out to Rp 1.46 miliar per unit, and the 2020 MBR-MYC record to Rp 74 juta per unit. Treat both as partial figures.
  • Low outliers. Three LPKB records in Jawa Timur (2022) work out to Rp 16 juta per unit, which looks like a partial scope (for example furniture or follow-on works) rather than full construction.
  • Repeated figures are standard type designs. Many projects share identical unit counts and costs at different sites (for example 14-unit LPKB blocks). They are separate buildings, not duplicate records.
  • No floor area. Without m² per unit, cost per unit mixes construction cost with unit size, so treat the comparisons as indicative.
  • Location precision depends on how the original records were geocoded. Dots show project locations, not building footprints.
